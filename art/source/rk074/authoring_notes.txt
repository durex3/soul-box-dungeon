RK-074 / Codex / 2026-10-30
60 native static Aseprite documents are the editable source of truth.
48 orbs: 12 SoulTable IDs x T1/T2/T3/T4. Five pickups and seven 8x8 tag icons.
RGB/sRGB, project ally palette, opaque pixels only. Each document has one Body layer;
T3/T4 also have a separate Decor layer. No animation, gameplay fields or baked background.

Native-grid recipes:
T1: 6x6 disk, solid tag fill. T2: native 8x8 disk and one bright core pixel.
T3: native 8x8 body at (2,1) on 10x10, core retained and two tail pixels in Decor.
Its pivot is (6,5), intentionally not the whole-canvas center (5,5).
T4: native 10x10 body at (1,1) on 12x12 with separated corner particle points.
No prior bitmap is read or resized to construct a new tier.
Dual tags color the left/right body halves in the formal table's exact order.
Icons use seven separate masks: flame, ice crystal, zigzag bolt, toothed gear,
eye-socket skull, hollow ring and crossed swords; grayscale/monochrome blind pages
are for project-manager visual review, not machine recognition claims.

History: initially authored with author_static.lua using native Aseprite Image/Cel APIs.
The small crystal's flat-top six-sided profile was refined in its native document,
and the historical recipe was updated to match. Other existing task assets untouched.
